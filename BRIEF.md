## The problem
- Older adults to seniors in my community struggle with finding finding ways to stay connected and avoid loneliness.
- Impact area: Environment
- Why it matters (impact): If my project works the older adults and seniors will be happier and have something to do.
- Evidence it’s real: I got the following paragraph: Human beings are social creatures. Our connection to others enables us to survive and thrive. Yet, as we age, many of us are alone more often than when we were younger, leaving us vulnerable to social isolation and loneliness—and related health problems such as cognitive decline, depression, and heart disease. Fortunately, there are ways to counteract these negative effects." 

From: https://www.nia.nih.gov/news/social-isolation-loneliness-older-people-pose-health-risks
- What people told me: Person A (my grandma): " Hi!, I haven't seen you in so long. When you are gone I don't even know what to do.

Person B (me): " Maybe you can introduce yourself to Ai and see what activities you can do!

## My solution
- My AI’s job: My AI helps by suggesting activities to elderly people, which works better than seniors searching up what they can do because the AI gives a immediate result while searching could take a longer time.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Senior Planet from AARP already exist. My project has translation so all elderlies could use it. The website will also ask questions. Based on their interest, the AI will suggest something to do for them.
- Safety plan: Risk: The AI ask a personal question

Plan: Tell AI not to ask ANY personal questions about the user

Risk: The user might accidentally click the wrong choice

Plan:  Add a back button in case the user clicks the wrong button

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)
1. The user can answer simple questions to give ideas to the ai so they can suggest a activity to do
2. The user can choose their preferred language
3. The user can choose a filter based on what they want( e.g. Indoor/outdoor)
4. The user can set a time of when they want to do the activity once they has set the time it should automatically set a alarm of the time chosen
5. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.

## Maybe later (not yet)
- The user can cancel the event anytime they want
- The user can set a time of when they want to do the activity

## My AI’s instructions
- When it answers: The user clicks a button called "Find A Activity" to make my AI answer
- Its job: My AI's job is to find a activity based on what the user had told the AI
- Who it talks to and how to sound: The AI is talking to an elderly person. The words and sentence structure should be simple and easy to understand.
- Facts it must use: The AI should use what the user's answer is
- Rules it must follow: The AI should never ask personal questions. Interest and name and other similar things are fine
- How to answer: The activity the AI gives the user should be simple and short easy enough for a elderly to understand
